## 🎯 Overview

This project tracks **sales performance** and **customer behavior** in two connected dashboards. Every KPI is compared year over year, and key months and weeks are highlighted so trends are easy to spot.

---

## 📊 Sales Dashboard

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **KPI overview** | Total sales, profit and quantity, CY vs PY |
| **Monthly trends** | Each KPI by month, with the highest and lowest sales months highlighted |
| **Subcategory comparison** | Sales and profit by product subcategory, CY vs PY |
| **Weekly trends** | Weekly sales and profit, with weeks above and below the average flagged |

## 👥 Customer Dashboard

| Section | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **KPI overview** | Total customers, sales per customer and number of orders, CY vs PY |
| **Monthly customer trends** | Customer counts by month, with peak and low months highlighted |
| **Order distribution** | Customers grouped by number of orders, showing loyalty and engagement |
| **Top 10 customers** | Ranked by profit, with orders, sales, profit and last order date |

## 🛠️ What I Used

| Category | Details |
|---|---|
| **Calculated fields** | CY and PY measures, % Diff |
| **Functions** | `SUM`, `COUNTD`, `IF`, `FIXED` LOD expressions and more |
| **Table calculations** | `WINDOW_MAX`, `WINDOW_MIN`, with Compute Using |
| **Visuals and interactivity** | Dual axis charts, filters, navigation between dashboards |
